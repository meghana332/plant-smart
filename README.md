# Plant Smart 🌳

**Team Electrobots · FIRST LEGO League Innovation Project**
*Plant smart, breathe easy!*

Plant Smart helps people at a plant nursery avoid buying "pollen bomb" trees. Scan a nursery tag or a leaf, and the app:

1. Identifies the tree or shrub (and whether it's a male, female or low-pollen variety)
2. Gives an allergy pollen score from 1 to 10 (in the spirit of the Ogren Plant Allergy Scale)
3. Shows a verdict: **Good choice**, **Think twice** or **Pollen bomb**
4. Suggests low-pollen trees to plant instead

## Use it
Open the site on a phone, tablet or laptop and add it to your home screen. After opening it once online, it works offline with the team's saved nursery tags and demo trees.

AI photo scanning uses a Claude API key that each device owner enters in **AI settings**. The key is stored only on that device and is never part of this code.

## Files
- `index.html` – the app
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js` – make it installable and offline-ready
- `icon-*.png` – app icons

Pollen scores are approximate. Always ask an arborist before planting or grafting.
